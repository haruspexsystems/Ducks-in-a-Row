Ducks in a Row

Certificate Authority is unavailable

HTTP 503 Service Unavailable
https://ducksinarow.dev/problems/ca-unavailable

What it means

Ducks in a Row could not reach the ADCS certification authority, so nothing was done. If you asked for a revocation, the certificate was not revoked.

A fresh installation answers this for every CA operation until the setup wizard has connected a CA.

What to do

Check that the Certificate Services service is running on the CA and that the Ducks in a Row server can reach it, then try again shortly. The troubleshooting guide covers both the unconfigured case and a CA that stopped answering.
