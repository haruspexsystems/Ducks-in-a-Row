Certificate Authority error

HTTP 500 Internal Server Error or 503 Service Unavailable
https://ducksinarow.dev/problems/ca-error

What it means

The certification authority refused or failed an operation, and Ducks in a Row could not put a more precise name to it. A revocation the CA refused answers 500; an unexpected failure while syncing the inventory or reading the template list answers 503.

What to do

After a refused revocation the inventory has already been refreshed, so check the certificate's current status first: the CA may have revoked it anyway, or may hold it under a different status. The service log on the Ducks in a Row server (C:\ProgramData\Ducks in a Row\logs\ducks-<date>.log) records the CA's own answer. Then try again.
