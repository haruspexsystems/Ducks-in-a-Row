CA access denied

HTTP 503 Service Unavailable
https://ducksinarow.dev/problems/ca-access-denied

What it means

The certification authority refused the Ducks in a Row service's own account for the operation you asked for. The response detail names the right that is missing and the operation that needs it: revoking a certificate needs Issue and Manage Certificates on the CA, and reading the certificate inventory needs Read.

The rights belong to the computer account of the server that runs Ducks in a Row, not to you at the browser.

What to do

If you want the operation to work, grant the named right to the server's computer account in the Certification Authority console (CA Properties, Security), then try again. The Settings page card Service rights on the CA shows what the account holds.

If you do not want the dashboard to revoke certificates, leave the right ungranted. Everything else keeps working.
