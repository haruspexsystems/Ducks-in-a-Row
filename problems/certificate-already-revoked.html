Certificate is already revoked

HTTP 409 Conflict
https://ducksinarow.dev/problems/certificate-already-revoked

What it means

The certification authority already lists this certificate as revoked, so Ducks in a Row sent no second revocation. Nothing changed at the CA.

What to do

Nothing is needed. Reload the certificate's page to see the status the CA holds.
