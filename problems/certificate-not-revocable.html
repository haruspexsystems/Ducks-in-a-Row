Ducks in a Row

Nothing to revoke

HTTP 409 Conflict
https://ducksinarow.dev/problems/certificate-not-revocable

What it means

The row you tried to revoke is a certificate request that never became a certificate, for example one the CA denied or one still waiting for approval. There is nothing at the certification authority to revoke.

What to do

Nothing is needed. A request still waiting for approval is approved or denied at the CA, in the Certification Authority console.
