Problem type

Outside the configured revocation scope

HTTP 403 Forbidden
https://ducksinarow.dev/problems/revocation-out-of-scope

What it means

The certificate passed the TLS guardrail, but the revocation scope set on the Settings page does not cover it. The default scope covers certificates Ducks in a Row obtained plus anything on a template enabled for ACME; a custom scope covers only the templates it lists.

What to do

Widen the scope on the Settings page if this certificate should be in reach, or revoke it at the CA in the Certification Authority console.

An ACME client revoking its own certificate is not subject to the scope.
